Privacy Policy

Ronini · Last updated 30 October 2026

Ronini keeps your baby's log on your phone. If you create an account, it is also backed up to a private account so it survives losing or changing your phone.

Only you can read it. It is never shared with other users, never sold, and never used for advertising. There is no analytics or tracking of any kind in this app.

What Ronini stores

Your email addressOnly if you create an account. Used only to sign you in, recover your password, and identify your data. Never used for marketing.
Baby profilesThe name and date of birth you enter for each baby, and your preferred weight and height units.
Feeding entriesBreastfeeding sessions, bottle and formula amounts, and their times.
Diaper entriesWet, dirty, or both, and their times.
Sleep entriesStart and end times of naps and night sleep.
Growth measurementsWeight, height, and head circumference.

Ronini does not ask for or collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health records from other apps, or any advertising identifier.

Where it is stored

Without an account, everything stays on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

With an account, on your device and in Google Firebase — Firebase Authentication for your sign-in and Cloud Firestore for your entries. Google acts as a data processor and stores the data on its cloud infrastructure. Firebase is used only for accounts and storage; Ronini does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or any advertising product.

Who can see it

Only you, signed in to your own account. Every record is stored under your account identifier, and the database security rules reject any request from a different account. There is no sharing between users and no multi-caregiver access — if you invite someone to Ronini, they get their own separate log and cannot see yours. Invitations are sent through the messaging or sharing app you choose; Ronini does not see or store who you invite.

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Reset all data deletes every baby profile and every logged entry.
  • Settings → Delete account asks for your password, then permanently deletes every entry, every baby profile, and the account itself.

Both are permanent and cannot be undone — there is no backup we can restore from, and signing up again with the same email will not bring anything back.

When you delete a single entry, a small marker holding only that entry's identifier and the time it was deleted is kept for up to 90 days. This exists so the deletion reaches your other devices instead of the entry reappearing. It contains none of the entry's contents, and deleting your account removes these markers along with everything else.

Keeping it secure

All traffic between the app and Firebase uses HTTPS. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never stored by Ronini or visible to us. Account deletion requires re-entering your password.

Children's privacy

Ronini is for parents and caregivers. The account holder must be an adult, and the app is not directed to or intended for use by children. Information about an infant is entered by the caregiver who holds the account, is visible only to that account, and is never used for profiling or advertising.

Changes to this policy

If Ronini gains features that change how data is handled — sharing a log between caregivers, for example — this policy will be updated before those features ship, and the date at the top will change.
